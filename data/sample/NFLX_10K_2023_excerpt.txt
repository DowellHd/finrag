ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors may materially harm our business, operating results, and financial condition.

Competition for Entertainment Time

We compete broadly for the leisure time of consumers against other streaming services, linear television, gaming, and social media. Increased competition could make it harder to acquire and retain paid memberships.

Content Costs and Production Risk

Our business depends on the continued investment in and successful execution of a broad slate of content. Production delays, including from industry-wide labor disruptions, and rising content costs could adversely affect our margins and member satisfaction.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights

Revenue grew 7% to $33.7 billion, compared with $31.6 billion in 2022. Global streaming paid memberships reached over 260 million by year end, with a record 29.6 million paid net additions during the year, aided by the continued rollout of paid sharing and the ad-supported plan. Operating margin expanded to 21%, up from 18% in 2022. Net income was $5.4 billion, up 20% year over year.

The ad-supported plan grew to more than 23 million monthly active users by the end of the fiscal year, and free cash flow reached a record $6.9 billion for the year.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents were $7.1 billion as of December 31, 2023. Free cash flow was $6.9 billion, up substantially from $1.6 billion in 2022. The company continued to reduce debt outstanding and did not repurchase shares during most of the fiscal year.
