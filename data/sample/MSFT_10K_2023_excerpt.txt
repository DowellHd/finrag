ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors may materially harm our business, operating results, and financial condition.

Competition and Technology Risks

The markets for our products and services are highly competitive, characterized by rapid technological change, and marked by evolving customer needs and frequent product introductions and improvements. Our competitors range from large, well-established companies to emerging startups. Our success is highly dependent on our ability to attract and retain talented employees, particularly in artificial intelligence and cloud computing, where competition for skilled personnel is intense.

Cybersecurity and Data Risks

Cyberattacks and security vulnerabilities could lead to reduced revenue, increased costs, liability claims, or harm to our reputation or competitive position. As a leading provider of cloud-based services, we are a frequent target of such attacks.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights

Revenue increased 7% to $211.9 billion, driven by growth across our cloud offerings, including Microsoft Cloud revenue of $111.6 billion, up 22%. Net income was $72.4 billion, and diluted earnings per share was $9.68.

Segment revenue: Productivity and Business Processes revenue was $69.3 billion. Intelligent Cloud revenue was $87.9 billion, driven by Azure and other cloud services growth of 27%. More Personal Computing revenue was $54.7 billion.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments were $111.3 billion as of June 30, 2023. Cash flow from operations was $87.6 billion. We returned $9.7 billion to shareholders in the form of share repurchases and dividends in the fourth quarter alone.
