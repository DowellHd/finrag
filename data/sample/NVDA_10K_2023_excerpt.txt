ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors may materially harm our business, operating results, and financial condition.

Demand and Supply Chain Volatility

Our results of operations have fluctuated in the past and may fluctuate significantly in the future due to changes in demand for our products, particularly in gaming and data center markets, and our reliance on third-party foundries and manufacturing partners for wafer supply.

Customer Concentration in Data Center

A significant portion of our data center revenue is concentrated among a small number of large cloud service providers. The loss of, or reduced purchasing by, any of these customers could harm our operating results.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights

Revenue was $26.97 billion, roughly flat compared with fiscal 2022, as growth in Data Center revenue of 41% was offset by a decline in Gaming revenue due to macroeconomic conditions and channel inventory corrections. Data Center revenue reached a record $15.0 billion for the fiscal year. Net income was $4.37 billion, down from $9.75 billion in fiscal 2022, reflecting inventory charges and lower Gaming demand.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $13.3 billion as of the end of fiscal 2023. Cash provided by operating activities was $5.6 billion. We returned $9.5 billion to shareholders through share repurchases and dividends during the fiscal year.
