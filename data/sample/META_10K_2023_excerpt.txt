ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors may materially harm our business, operating results, and financial condition.

Advertising Revenue Concentration

We generate substantially all of our revenue from advertising. Our advertising revenue could be adversely affected by changes in user engagement, platform policy changes at other companies (e.g. mobile operating systems), macroeconomic conditions, and evolving privacy regulation limiting ad targeting.

Reality Labs and Long-Term Investment Risk

We continue to invest heavily in Reality Labs, including augmented and virtual reality hardware and the metaverse. These investments have resulted in significant operating losses and may not generate a return, or may take many years to do so.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights

Revenue increased 16% to $134.9 billion, compared with $116.6 billion in 2022, driven by growth in ad impressions and the impact of AI investments on ad performance. Family of Apps revenue was approximately $133.2 billion with operating income of $62.9 billion. Reality Labs revenue was $1.9 billion with an operating loss of $16.1 billion. Net income was $39.1 billion, more than double the $23.2 billion reported in 2022.

Daily active people (DAP) across our Family of Apps averaged 3.19 billion in December 2023, up 8% year over year.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $65.4 billion as of December 31, 2023. Cash flow from operations was $71.1 billion. The company repurchased $19.7 billion of Class A common stock and initiated its first-ever quarterly dividend in early 2024.
