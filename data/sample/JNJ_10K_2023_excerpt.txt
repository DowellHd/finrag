ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors may materially harm our business, operating results, and financial condition.

Product Liability and Litigation

We are involved in numerous product liability lawsuits, including litigation related to talc-based products, that could result in substantial settlements, judgments, or reputational harm regardless of the merits of the claims.

Patent Expirations and Competition

Our pharmaceutical business faces the loss of patent protection and exclusivity for several key products, which could result in generic or biosimilar competition and reduced revenue.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights

Worldwide sales from continuing operations (following the November 2023 Kenvue consumer health spin-off) were $85.2 billion, up 6.5% versus the prior year. Innovative Medicine sales were $54.6 billion, and MedTech sales were $30.5 billion. Net earnings attributable to Johnson & Johnson were $35.2 billion, which included a significant one-time non-cash gain related to the Kenvue separation.

Oncology and immunology continued to be the largest growth drivers within Innovative Medicine, while MedTech benefited from continued recovery in surgical procedure volumes.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents were $24.9 billion as of December 31, 2023. Cash flow from operations was $19.8 billion. The company paid $11.8 billion in dividends and remains committed to its long-standing annual dividend increase.
