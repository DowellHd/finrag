ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors may materially harm our business, operating results, and financial condition.

Commodity Price Volatility

Our results of operations and cash flows are heavily influenced by crude oil and natural gas prices, which are subject to significant volatility driven by global supply and demand, OPEC+ decisions, and geopolitical events.

Energy Transition and Climate Regulation

Government policies and regulations addressing climate change, including carbon pricing and emissions standards, could increase our operating costs and affect demand for our products over the long term.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights

Total revenue and other income was $344.6 billion, compared with $413.7 billion in 2022, reflecting lower natural gas and crude prices compared with the prior year's highs. Net income was $36.0 billion, compared with a record $55.7 billion in 2022. Full-year production averaged 3.8 million oil-equivalent barrels per day, including the Pioneer Natural Resources merger completed in 2024.

Upstream earnings were $22.5 billion, and Energy Products earnings were $9.9 billion, reflecting normalized refining margins compared with 2022's exceptional levels.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents were $31.5 billion as of December 31, 2023. Cash flow from operations was $55.4 billion. The company distributed $32.4 billion to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases during 2023.
