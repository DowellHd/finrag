ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors may materially harm our business, operating results, and financial condition.

Advertising Revenue Concentration

We generate the substantial majority of our revenues from advertising. Adverse changes in the advertising market, changes in user behavior, or new privacy regulations affecting our ability to target advertisements could harm our revenue.

Competition in Search and AI

We face growing competition in search, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence from companies that may develop competing products or invest more aggressively in generative AI capabilities.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights

Revenues increased 9% to $307.4 billion, compared with $282.8 billion in 2022. Google Search and other advertising revenue was approximately $175.0 billion, YouTube ads revenue was $31.5 billion, and Google Cloud revenue was $33.1 billion, up 26%. Net income was $73.8 billion, up 23% year over year.

Google Cloud reported its first full year of profitability, with operating income of $1.7 billion, compared with an operating loss in 2022.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $110.9 billion as of December 31, 2023. Cash generated from operations was $101.7 billion. We repurchased $61.5 billion of our Class A and Class C shares during 2023.
