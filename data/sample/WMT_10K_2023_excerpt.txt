ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors may materially harm our business, operating results, and financial condition.

Margin Pressure and Inflation

Our business is sensitive to changes in economic conditions, including inflation, which can affect consumer spending patterns and increase our costs for merchandise, transportation, and labor faster than we are able to pass those costs on to customers.

E-commerce and Omnichannel Execution

We continue to invest heavily in e-commerce, supply chain automation, and last-mile delivery capabilities. Failure to execute this strategy effectively relative to competitors could harm market share and margins.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights

Total revenue was $611.3 billion, up 6.7% versus the prior fiscal year, driven by strength in Walmart U.S. and Sam's Club, as well as continued growth in global e-commerce, which grew 17%. Net income attributable to Walmart was $11.68 billion, compared with $13.67 billion in the prior fiscal year, reflecting a legal settlement charge related to opioid litigation.

Walmart U.S. comparable sales grew 6.6% for the fiscal year, and membership income at Sam's Club grew 7.7%.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash and cash equivalents were $9.9 billion as of January 31, 2023. Cash flow from operations was $28.8 billion. The company paid $6.1 billion in dividends and repurchased $9.9 billion of shares during the fiscal year.
