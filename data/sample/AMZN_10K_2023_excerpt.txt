ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors may materially harm our business, operating results, and financial condition.

Intense Competition and Thin Margins

We face intense competition across all of our businesses, including retail, third-party marketplaces, and cloud computing. Our businesses operate at low profit margins, and we make significant investments in new products and services that may not prove successful.

Inventory and Fulfillment Risks

We are exposed to risks associated with inventory management, fulfillment network capacity, and reliance on third-party sellers and delivery service partners. Disruptions to our fulfillment or transportation networks could harm our reputation and profitability.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Fiscal Year 2023 Highlights

Net sales increased 12% to $574.8 billion, compared with $514.0 billion in 2022. North America segment sales were $352.8 billion, International segment sales were $131.2 billion, and AWS segment sales were $90.8 billion, up 13%. Net income was $30.4 billion, compared with a net loss of $2.7 billion in 2022.

Operating income increased to $36.9 billion, compared with $12.2 billion in 2022, driven by cost efficiencies across the fulfillment network and continued AWS growth.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $73.4 billion as of December 31, 2023. Cash provided by operating activities increased to $84.9 billion. Capital expenditures were $48.1 billion, reflecting continued investment in fulfillment and technology infrastructure, including AWS data centers.
